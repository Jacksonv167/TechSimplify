Best Free AI Video Generators in 2026

AI video generators make it easier to create videos from text, images and simple instructions. This beginner-friendly guide explains how AI video tools work and how you can start using them.

What Is an AI Video Generator?

An AI video generator is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to help create or edit videos.

Depending on the tool, you may be able to create videos from text prompts, images or existing video clips.

1. Kling AI

Kling AI is an AI video generation platform that can create videos from text descriptions and images.

It can be useful for creating cinematic scenes, animated sequences and creative short videos.

Why Try Kling AI?

  • Text-to-video generation
  • Image-to-video generation
  • Useful for creative projects
  • Suitable for experimenting with AI video

2. Runway

Runway is an AI-powered creative platform that provides tools for generating and editing video.

It can be useful for creators who want to experiment with AI-generated video and visual effects.

3. Pika

Pika is another AI video creation platform that allows users to experiment with generating and transforming video content.

It can be useful for short creative clips and social media content.

How to Create a Video With AI

The exact process depends on the tool, but the basic steps are usually similar.

  1. Open an AI video generation platform.
  2. Choose a text-to-video or image-to-video feature.
  3. Write a description of the video you want.
  4. Generate the video.
  5. Review the result and make changes if necessary.
  6. Export the finished video when the tool allows it.

Example AI Video Prompt

A detailed prompt can help the AI understand the scene you want to create.

A cinematic view of a busy Lagos street at sunset, people walking naturally, cars moving slowly, realistic lighting, smooth camera movement and detailed surroundings.

How to Make Better AI Videos

  • Describe the scene clearly.
  • Explain what the characters should do.
  • Describe the camera movement.
  • Mention the lighting and atmosphere.
  • Keep your first prompts simple.
  • Experiment with different versions.

Can You Create AI Videos for Free?

Many AI video platforms offer free trials, free credits or limited free generations. However, free limits and available features can change over time.

Some platforms may also place restrictions on video length, resolution or downloads.

Who Can Use AI Video Generators?

AI video generators can be useful for beginners, content creators, students, marketers and social media users.

You do not necessarily need advanced video-editing skills to experiment with AI video tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free AI video generator?

There is no single best option for everyone. The right tool depends on the type of video you want to create and the features and free limits available at the time.

Can AI turn an image into a video?

Yes. Some AI video platforms provide image-to-video features that animate a still image based on your instructions.

Can AI videos be used on social media?

Yes, AI-generated videos can be used for many types of social media content, subject to the platform's rules and the AI tool's licensing terms.

Conclusion

AI video generators are making video creation easier for beginners and experienced creators. Start with simple prompts, experiment with different tools and gradually learn how to create more detailed scenes.
